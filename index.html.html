The project

About the archive

Bangtan Archive is an independent fan-made project documenting BTS's musical history in one place, organized by year. It aims to include official group releases, member solo releases, mixtapes, covers, pre-debut material, SoundCloud uploads, collaborations, OSTs, remixes, alternate language versions, demos and other notable music-related releases.

Entries should identify what kind of release they are and link to a reliable source. Official releases, member-posted material, historical pre-debut recordings, and fan reuploads are different categories and should never be presented as interchangeable.

This unofficial fan project is not affiliated with BTS, BIGHIT MUSIC, or HYBE. Song names and music belong to their respective rights holders. Links should direct visitors to authorized sources where available.
